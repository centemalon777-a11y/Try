Official JAMB Set Novel

The Lekki Headmaster

Written by Kabir Alabi Garba

The story follows Mr. Bepo, a respected school principal (headmaster), and explores the challenges, experiences, and realities of life in modern Nigeria — particularly in the Lekki area of Lagos. A deeply inspiring story of leadership, sacrifice, and community.

📌 JAMB UTME 2025/2026

What the Novel Teaches

JAMB sets questions from this novel every year. Students are expected to know the characters, key events, chapter summaries, themes, and lessons — and be able to answer exam-style questions under timed conditions.
